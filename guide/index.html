Spendify User Guide

Everything the app can do, explained in plain language.

Spendify has five tabs along the bottom of the screen, and a menu at the top right for everything else. If you are new, start with Getting Started — it walks through your first few minutes with the app.

Guides

Getting Started Signing in, finding your way around, and adding your first expense. Adding Expenses Typing or speaking an expense, choosing categories, splitting a bill, and setting up recurring bills. Budgets Setting a limit, choosing a cycle, carrying money forward, and what "Safe to Spend" means. Groups & Shared Expenses Creating a group, inviting people, splitting costs, and settling up. Wishlist Goals Saving towards something, and setting a reminder so you don't forget. AI Features Voice entry, asking questions in any language, and the summaries the app writes for you. Settings, Backup & Accessibility Text size, dark mode, app lock, online backup, and exporting your data.

Quick reference: the five tabs

TabWhat it's for
HomeYour recent expenses, totals, and AI summaries.
ExpenseAdding a new expense, by typing or speaking.
BucketThings you're saving towards, with optional reminders.
BudgetYour spending limit and how much is left.
GroupsShared expenses with family, flatmates or friends.
