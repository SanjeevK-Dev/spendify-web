What Spendify does

  • Speak your expenses Say what you spent and Spendify works out the amount and category for you. It understands English, Hindi, and other languages.
  • Scan a receipt Point your camera at a receipt and Spendify reads it for you, splitting it into item-wise expenses instead of you typing each one out. Works for your own expenses and for group expenses too.
  • Split bills with groups Share expenses with family, flatmates or friends. Spendify works out who owes what, and settles up over UPI.
  • Budgets that make sense Set a monthly limit, carry unspent money forward, and get a warning before your cycle ends.
  • Ask questions in plain language "Where did I spend the most?" — ask by typing or speaking, and hear the answer read back to you.
  • Built to be accessible Full screen reader support, adjustable text size, and a dark mode. Every control is properly labelled and sized.
