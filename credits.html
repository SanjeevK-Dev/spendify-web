Thank you

A great deal of what works well in Spendify exists because someone took the trouble to try it, notice what was wrong, and say so. Testing is slow, repetitive work, and bugs found early are the ones that never reach anyone else. Avinash and Rohit did a lot of that work, and the app is considerably better for it.

Thanks also to everyone who has used Spendify and sent feedback. Every report of something confusing, broken or hard to reach has fed back into the app.

Built with

Spendify is made possible by these tools and services:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, cloud storage and syncing
  • Google Gemini — the AI behind voice entry, summaries and Ask AI
  • Material Design — the interface components
  • Android Jetpack — the app's underlying architecture
